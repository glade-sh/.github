# Glade Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Glade respectful and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on behavior, code, evidence, and outcomes—not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and avoid sharing proprietary source, credentials,
  customer records, or unredacted support bundles.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in Glade repositories and community spaces, and when a
person is officially representing the project in public or private.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[conduct@glade.sh](mailto:conduct@glade.sh). Do not open a public issue for a
private conduct report. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with
confidentiality appropriate to the circumstances.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, impose a
temporary ban, or permanently remove someone from project spaces. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is not permitted.

For security vulnerabilities, use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting route or [security@glade.sh](mailto:security@glade.sh).
